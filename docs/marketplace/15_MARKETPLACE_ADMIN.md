# 15. MARKETPLACE ADMIN & MODERATION SPECIFICATION

> **Target App**: `athlon-admin`  
> **Status**: SPECIFIED  

---

## 1. Administration Modules
- **Listing Moderation**: Approve, reject, or request changes on pending listings.
- **Shop Verification**: Review business documentation, store address, and assign verified retailer seals.
- **Commission Monitoring**: View snapshotted gross sales, platform commissions, and seller balances.
- **Reporting Console**: Review user flags for fake items, inaccurate condition descriptions, or policy violations.
