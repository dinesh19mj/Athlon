# 10. SPORTS SHOP SUBSCRIPTION SPECIFICATION

> **Plan**: `MARKETPLACE_SHOP`  
> **Status**: SPECIFIED

## 1. Plan Overview
- Designed for authorized retail sports dealers and pro shops.
- **Entitlements**:
  - `MARKET_SHOP_CREATION`: Registered verified shop profile and public storefront.
  - `MARKET_INVENTORY_MANAGEMENT`: SKU, multi-quantity, and low-stock alerts.
  - `MARKET_ORDER_MANAGEMENT`: Dispatch tracking, customer enquiries, and offer approvals.
  - `MARKET_UNLIMITED_PRODUCTS`: Unlimited active product catalog.
  - `MARKET_ANALYTICS`: Daily revenue telemetry and customer conversion insights.
