# 08. SELLER SUBSCRIPTION SPECIFICATION

> **Plan**: `MARKETPLACE_SELLER`  
> **Status**: SPECIFIED  

---

## 1. Plan Overview
- Designed for active equipment traders who do not meet the 3-tournament requirement or who wish to list commercial quantities of pre-owned sports gear.
- **Entitlements**:
  - `MARKET_SELL`: Unlocks instant listing capability.
  - `MARKET_ELIGIBILITY_BYPASS`: Bypasses 3-tournament requirement.
  - `MARKET_LISTING_LIMIT`: 25 concurrent active listings.
  - `MARKET_COMMISSION_DISCOUNT`: Discounted platform fee (2.5% vs standard 5%).
  - `MARKET_FEATURED_LISTING`: Priority badge and placement in search results.
